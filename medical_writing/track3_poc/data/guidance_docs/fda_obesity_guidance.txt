FDA Guidance for Industry
Developing Products for Weight Management (Revision 1)
Synthetic document for Track 3 POC — not an actual FDA guidance.

Section 1 — Scope
This guidance describes the Agency's current thinking on the development of
drug products for chronic weight management in adults with obesity.

Section 3.1 — Clinical Trial Design
Sponsors should include a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
design for Phase 2 obesity studies. Trials should be of adequate duration to
characterise weight loss and its maintenance.

Section 4.2 — Endpoints
The primary endpoint for obesity trials should be percent change from baseline
in body weight. A weight loss threshold responder analysis (proportion of
patients achieving at least 5% weight loss) should be included as a key
secondary endpoint.

Section 5.1 — Safety
Cardiovascular safety must be addressed, including monitoring for heart rate,
blood pressure, and major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE). An independent
adjudication committee is recommended.

Section 6.0 — Phase 3 Design
The End-of-Phase-2 briefing document should include a detailed description of
the proposed Phase 3 design, including sample size justification and an
off-treatment follow-up period to characterise weight regain.
